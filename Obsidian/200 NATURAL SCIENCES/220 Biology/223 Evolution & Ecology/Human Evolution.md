## 1. Overview & Taxonomy

The family **Hominidae** includes the **hominoids** (great apes and humans). Molecular evidence indicates humans and chimpanzees diverged from a common ancestor ~**6 MYA** (Million Years Ago).

> [!INFO] Definition: Hominin
> 
> The term **Hominin** refers to species that evolved _after_ the split from the chimpanzee lineage. They are more closely related to humans than chimps.
> 
> **Key Distinctions:** Bipedalism (upright posture), increased brain size, and fully opposable thumbs.

![[Pasted image 20260202235915.png|center|550]]

**[^1]Phylogeny:**
Evolution is not a straight line. It is a "bushy" tree where many species co-existed, and many branches (like _Paranthropus_) were evolutionary dead ends.

## 3. Genus [[Australopithecus]] (4 - 2 MYA)

"Southern Ape." Likely ancestors to genus _Homo_. They evolved in East Africa.

![[Pasted image 20260203000926.png|center|400]]

- **Characteristics:**
	
    - **Bipedal:** Confirmed by Laetoli footprints (3.6 MYA).
        
    - **Sexual Dimorphism:** Males 50% larger than females (similar to gorillas).
        
    - **Cranial:** Smaller brains (similar to apes), prognathic (protruding) jaws.
        
- **Key Species:**
    
    - _A. anamnesis_ (4.2 MYA).
	        ![[Pasted image 20260203000826.png|400]]
    - _A. africanus_ (2-3 MYA): Larger brain (500cc), slender build.
	        ![[Pasted image 20260203000850.png|400]]
    - **Australopithecus afarensis** (3.9 - 2.9 MYA):
	        ![[Pasted image 20260203000631.png]]
        - Reduced dentition compared to apes.
            
        - **"Lucy"**: Famous 3.24 MYA fossil (40% complete skeleton) found in Ethiopia.
             ![[Pasted image 20260203000734.png|350]]

## 4. Genus [[Paranthropus]] (2.5 MYA - Dead End)

A distinct evolutionary branch that went extinct ~1 MYA. They are **not** ancestral to modern humans.
	![[Pasted image 20260203001021.png|center|500]]
- **Physiology:** Robust, muscular build (1.3-1.4m tall).
    
- **Diet:** Large grinding molars and heavy wear suggest a coarse, fibrous vegetarian diet.
    
- **Species:** _P. robustus_ (S. Africa), _P. aethiopicus_ & _P. boisei_ (E. Africa).
	
    ![[Pasted image 20260203001050.png]]

## 5. Genus [[Homo]] (2.5 MYA - Present)

The human genus. Characterized by larger brains, reduced jaws, and tool use.

![[Pasted image 20260203001208.png|center|550]]
### A. Early Homo

- **[[Homo habilis]]** ("Handy Man"):
	    ![[Pasted image 20260203001517.png|center|400]]
    - 2.5 - 3 MYA.
        
    - Brain: 600-750cc.
        
    - Artifacts: Associated with stone tools.
         ![[Pasted image 20260203001557.png]]
         
- **[[Homo erectus]]**:
	 ![[Pasted image 20260203001705.png|center|400]]
    - ~1.8 MYA - 50,000 YA.
        
    - **Firsts:** First to migrate out of Africa (found in India, China, Europe), use fire, hunt, and have home bases.
        
    - **Anatomy:** Human-like height and sexual dimorphism; downward-facing nostrils (adaptation for cold air).
	    
        ![[Pasted image 20260203001751.png]]

### B. Archaic Humans

Appeared ~500k years ago. Evolved from _H. erectus_.

![[Pasted image 20260203001913.png|center|500]]

- **Species:** _H. heidelbergensis_, _H. rhodesiensis_, _H. neanderthalensis_ ([[Neanderthals]]).
    
- **Traits:** Modern brain size (1200-1400cc) but thick skulls, prominent brow ridges, and receding chins.
	
	![[Pasted image 20260203001851.png]]

### C. [[Homo sapiens]] (Modern Humans)

- **The "Out of Africa" Theory:** Modern humans arose in Africa from _H. erectus_ and migrated out in a second wave ~100,000 years ago, replacing older species.
	
	![[Pasted image 20260203002301.png]]
	
	![[Pasted image 20260203002435.png|center|450]]
	
- **Denisovans:** A distinct subspecies identified via DNA (teeth/finger bones). Shared a common ancestor with Neanderthals.

## 6. Molecular Evidence

Genetic analysis supports the fossil record timeline.

> [!ABSTRACT] Genetic Divergence Timelines
> 
> - **Mitochondrial DNA (mtDNA):** Passed only through mothers. Suggests a common female ancestor ("Mitochondrial Eve") in Africa **~160,000 years ago**.
>     
> - **Y Chromosome:** Passed only father to son. Suggests a common male ancestor in Africa **~140,000 years ago**.
>     
> - **Interbreeding:** Genomes show modern humans interbred with both **Neanderthals** and **Denisovans**.
