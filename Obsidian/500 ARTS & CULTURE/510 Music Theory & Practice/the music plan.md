**Three structural decisions worth noting:**

**Basic Techniques** is its own note as the beginner entry point — it's where someone starts before they're ready for Legato, Tapping, or Harmonics. It links forward to the more advanced notes rather than being merged into them. This preserves a learning path.

**Rhythm** earns its own subfolder with 2 notes — Fundamentals (strumming mechanics) and Styles (blues, percussive). 8 videos is enough content, and rhythm is genuinely a different skill from technique.

**Harmonics** now has a natural progression inside one note: Natural → Harp → Pinch → Octave → Koto. The beginner Natural Harmonics video becomes the intro section of that note rather than a separate entry.
