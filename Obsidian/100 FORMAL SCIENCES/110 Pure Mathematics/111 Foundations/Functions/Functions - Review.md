### **Function Notation** 
> see: [[100 FORMAL SCIENCES/110 Pure Mathematics/111 Foundations/Functions/Functions]]

If $y = f(x)$:  
- $x$ = independent variable (input)  
- $y$ = dependent variable (output)  
- Domain = set of all possible inputs $x$  
- Range = set of all possible outputs $y$

---

### **Net Change**
> see: [[100 FORMAL SCIENCES/110 Pure Mathematics/111 Foundations/Functions/Functions]]

Between $x = a$ and $x = b$:  
$$
\text{net change} = f(b) - f(a)
$$

---

### **The Graph of a Function** 
> see [[The Graph of a Function]]

Graph of $f$ is the graph of equation $y = f(x)$.

---

### **Vertical Line Test** 
> see [[The Graph of a Function]]

A curve is a function’s graph **iff** no vertical line intersects it more than once.

---

### **Relations and Functions** 
> see: [[100 FORMAL SCIENCES/110 Pure Mathematics/111 Foundations/Functions/Functions]]

- Relation: any collection of ordered pairs $(x, y)$  
- Function: relation where each input $x$ has exactly one output $y$

---

### **Increasing and Decreasing Functions**
> see: [[Properties of Functions]]

On an interval:  
- $f$ increasing if $x_1 < x_2 \Rightarrow f(x_1) < f(x_2)$  
- $f$ decreasing if $x_1 < x_2 \Rightarrow f(x_1) > f(x_2)$

---

### **Local Maximum and Minimum Values**
> see: [[Properties of Functions]]

- $f(a)$ is local max if $f(a) \ge f(x)$ for all $x$ near $a$ → local maximum at $x = a$  
- $f(b)$ is local min if $f(b) \le f(x)$ for all $x$ near $b$ → local minimum at $x = b$

---

### **Average Rate of Change**
> see: [[Average Rate of Change of a Function]]

Between $x = a$ and $x = b$:  
$$
\text{average rate of change} = \frac{f(b) - f(a)}{b - a}
$$  
= slope of secant line through $(a, f(a))$ and $(b, f(b))$

---

### **Linear Functions**
> see: [[Library of Functions]] and [[Lines; Slopes]]

Form: $f(x) = ax + b$  
- Graph: straight line  
- Slope $a$ = constant average rate of change between any two points  
- $a =$ slope of graph of $f =$ rate of change of $f$

---

### **Vertical and Horizontal Shifts** 
> see: [[Transformations of Functions]]

Let $c > 0$:  

| Transformation | Effect |
|----------------|--------|
| $y = f(x) + c$ | Shift up $c$ units |
| $y = f(x) - c$ | Shift down $c$ units |
| $y = f(x - c)$ | Shift right $c$ units |
| $y = f(x + c)$ | Shift left $c$ units |

---

### **Reflecting Graphs** 
> see: [[Transformations of Functions]]

| Transformation | Reflection Axis |
|----------------|------------------|
| $y = -f(x)$    | About x-axis     |
| $y = f(-x)$    | About y-axis     |

---

### **Vertical Stretching/Shrinking** 
> see: [[Transformations of Functions]]

For $y = cf(x)$, $c > 0$:  
- If $c > 1$: stretch vertically by factor $c$  
- If $0 < c < 1$: shrink vertically by factor $c$

---

### **Horizontal Stretching/Shrinking**
> see: [[Transformations of Functions]]

For $y = f(cx)$, $c > 0$:  
- If $c > 1$: shrink horizontally by factor $1/c$  
- If $0 < c < 1$: stretch horizontally by factor $1/c$

---

### **Even and Odd Functions** 
> see: [[Properties of Functions]]

- Even: $f(-x) = f(x)$ → symmetric about y-axis  
- Odd: $f(-x) = -f(x)$ → symmetric about origin

---

### **Composition of Functions** 
> see: [[Combining Functions]]

$(f \circ g)(x) = f(g(x))$  
Domain: all $x$ in domain of $g$ such that $g(x)$ is in domain of $f$

---

### **One-to-One Functions**
> see: [[One-to-One Functions and Their Inverses]]

$f$ is one-to-one (injective) if:  
$$
x_1 \ne x_2 \Rightarrow f(x_1) \ne f(x_2)
$$  
Equivalently: $f(x_1) = f(x_2) \Rightarrow x_1 = x_2$

---

### **Horizontal Line Test** 
> see: [[One-to-One Functions and Their Inverses]]

$f$ is one-to-one **iff** no horizontal line intersects its graph more than once.

---

### **Inverse of a Function** 
> see: [[One-to-One Functions and Their Inverses]]

If $f$ is one-to-one with domain $A$, range $B$, then inverse $f^{-1}$ has:  
- Domain = $B$, Range = $A$  
- Defined by: $f^{-1}(y) = x \iff f(x) = y$

Cancellation equations:  
$$
f^{-1}(f(x)) = x \quad \forall x \in A \\
f(f^{-1}(x)) = x \quad \forall x \in B
$$
