A quick reference guide for the key formulas and equations from this chapter.

## 1. Essential Formulas

> [!info] Distance Formula
> Distance between $P_1(x_1, y_1)$ and $P_2(x_2, y_2)$:
> $$ d = \sqrt{(x_2 - x_1)^2 + (y_2 - y_1)^2} $$

> [!info] Midpoint Formula
> Midpoint of the segment connecting $(x_1, y_1)$ and $(x_2, y_2)$:
> $$ M = \left( \frac{x_1 + x_2}{2}, \frac{y_1 + y_2}{2} \right) $$

> [!info] Slope Formula
> Slope $m$ of a non-vertical line through $(x_1, y_1)$ and $(x_2, y_2)$:
> $$ m = \frac{y_2 - y_1}{x_2 - x_1} $$
> *Note: Undefined if $x_1 = x_2$ (Vertical Line).*

> [!tip] Line Relationships
> *   **Parallel Lines:** Slopes are equal ($m_1 = m_2$) and y-intercepts differ ($b_1 \neq b_2$).
> *   **Perpendicular Lines:** Product of slopes is $-1$ ($m_1 \cdot m_2 = -1$).

## 2. Equations of Lines

| Type of Line | Equation | Key Parameters |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Vertical Line** | $x = a$ | $a$ is the x-intercept. |
| **Horizontal Line** | $y = b$ | $b$ is the y-intercept. |
| **Point-Slope Form** | $y - y_1 = m(x - x_1)$ | Uses point $(x_1, y_1)$ and slope $m$. |
| **Slope-Intercept Form** | $y = mx + b$ | Uses slope $m$ and y-intercept $b$. |
| **General Form** | $Ax + By = C$ | $A, B$ not both 0. |

## 3. Equations of Circles

> [!info] Standard Form of a Circle
> Circle with center $(h, k)$ and radius $r$:
> $$ (x - h)^2 + (y - k)^2 = r^2 $$

> [!info] Unit Circle
> Circle centered at origin with radius 1:
> $$ x^2 + y^2 = 1 $$

> [!warning] General Form of a Circle
> Equation of the form:
> $$ x^2 + y^2 + ax + by + c = 0 $$
> *Note: This may represent a circle, a single point, or no graph at all depending on the values of $a, b, c$.*
